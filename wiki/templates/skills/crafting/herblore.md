# {icon} Herblore

Brew potions from farming crops and dungeon reagents. Potions give combat stat boosts that scale with the corresponding unboosted skill level and can be selected before entering a dungeon or boss fight.

Adding any ash as a catalyst produces an **enhanced** potion. Ash tier makes no difference: the full flat-plus-percentage bonus is doubled after rounding down. At level 99, Overload grants +24 to each of Attack, Strength, Defense, Ranged and Magic; enhanced Overload grants +48. Prestige potency adds its flat bonus afterward.

Magic, Super Magic and Overload also increase spell-and-equipment damage by 3%, 6% and 10%, respectively (6%, 12% and 20% when enhanced). Damage is rounded down. One potion lasts the combat session.

Enhanced potions count as their base potion for quests, guild tasks, seasonal tasks and Sell Junk protection. Higher-tier substitutes, including enhanced substitutes, also count toward applicable guild requests.

## Mortars and pestles

{mortar_table}

Smith Bronze through Runite mortars from two matching bars. Smithing governs production; Herblore governs equipping. Dwarven and Divine mortars come from the existing quest reward pools. Brood Empress independently drops the Heirloom Mortar and Pestle at 1/100, skipping it when already owned.

Tool efficiency increases XP per potion and reduces brewing time. It does not increase potion quantity or strength. The heirloom grows from 1.25× to 4.50× efficiency as it earns Herblore XP, with full potential requiring item level 99 and Herblore 85. Item XP survives prestige.
