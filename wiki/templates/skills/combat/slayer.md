# {icon} Slayer

Visit the **Slayer Master** in Town to receive a task. Hunt the assigned enemy in any dungeon that contains them. **Every matching kill in a session awards Slayer XP**, even after you meet the task's kill quota. The quota controls task completion and points, not the session's XP limit.

For example, a task requiring 14 Dragons still awards XP for all 160 Dragons if you kill that many in the session. It awards completion points once. If you do not reach the quota, your progress carries into the next session. All qualifying kills count toward on-task kill quests.

Slayer rewards and task progression are resolved when sessions are collected, oldest first. A new target takes over for the next collected session. Once your assignments run out, later sessions earn no Slayer XP until you obtain another assignment. Dungeon kills before death retain Slayer credit; failed Tower floors do not award Slayer credit.

## Points

Completing a task awards **Slayer points** (more for higher-level tasks). Points can be spent in the **Slayer Shop** on:

- XP Lamp (Small) — 50 pts, +10,000 XP to any skill
- XP Lamp (Large) — 200 pts, +50,000 XP to any skill
- Abyssal Whip — 300 pts (exclusive gear)
- Slayer Helm — 400 pts (exclusive gear)
- Slayer Platebody — 350 pts (exclusive gear)
- Slayer Platelegs — 250 pts (exclusive gear)
- Slayer Plateskirt — 250 pts (exclusive gear)

Use **Skip (30 pts)** to discard a task you don't want.

## Foretell

Spend bones at the Slayer Master to **foretell** upcoming tasks - queue up to three future assignments so they're ready when the current one ends. Each queued slot costs more than the last: 10 bone-units for the first, 25 for the second, and 50 for the third, where a regular bone counts as one unit and higher-tier bones count for more. Your cheapest bones are spent first.

Excess kills can finish consecutive assignments for the same enemy, awarding each assignment's points. A different target in the queue waits until the next session. Duplicate assignments never award XP twice for the same kill.

The **Foresight** prestige path extends this. Its first three nodes raise the queue cap by +1, +2, then +3 (up to six foretold tasks at once). Its final node lets all matching foretold targets earn session-wide XP and task progress, regardless of queue order. Each kill still earns XP once and contributes to only one assignment's quota.
